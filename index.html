How it works. A GitHub Action refreshes the schedule (NHL API), ESPN/DraftKings moneylines and totals, probable goalies, Jupiter prediction-market prices and season results every hour from 10 AM to 11 PM ET. When you press Simulate the page also re-pulls live odds and goalies from ESPN and live prices from Jupiter, then runs the selected model for every game. v4-independent (default) uses no betting-market input: a Poisson regression on exponentially weighted goals, MoneyPuck expected goals and shots for/against, the starting goalie's goals-saved-vs-expected, home ice, rest/back-to-backs and travel (refit every update on 2022-23 onward); if the confirmed goalie differs from the one the Action used, the page re-weights with that goalie's rating. v3 instead fits the de-vigged DraftKings moneyline and total exactly. Each game is simulated with score effects, goalie pulls/empty nets and 3-on-3 OT/shootout, and the pick is the non-tied score with the lowest expected |away error| + |home error| (OT/SO winner gets +1). The OT label is shown when P(OT | that score) ≥ 55%. Without odds the model falls back to team ratings.

Best bet. For every Jupiter market on the game (moneyline; totals and puck lines too if Jupiter lists them — as of Oct 2026 it lists NHL moneylines only) the model probability q is compared with the ask p: EV per $1 = q / (p + fee) − 1. A bet is recommended only if q > 50% and EV after fee > 0; the best bet is the qualifying side with the highest EV, otherwise the game is NO BET. The same rules drive the Discord /nhl command.

Jupiter edge. Jupiter NHL game markets (Polymarket-sourced) resolve on the final score including overtime and shootouts, so they are compared with the model's win probability including OT/SO. Price = YES ask (cost of one $1 contract). Edge = model % − Jupiter ask; "no-vig" divides by the sum of both asks. EV per $1 = model / (ask + fee) − 1 with fee = 0.07 × p × (1 − p) per contract (Jupiter's published fee table; rounded up to the cent per order).
