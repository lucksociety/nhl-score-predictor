How it works. A GitHub Action refreshes the schedule (NHL API), ESPN/DraftKings moneylines and totals, probable goalies, Polymarket prediction-market prices and season results every hour from 10 AM to 11 PM ET. When you press Simulate the page also re-pulls live odds and goalies from ESPN and live prices from Polymarket, then runs the selected model for every game. v4-independent (default) uses no betting-market input: a Poisson regression on goals, MoneyPuck expected goals and shots for/against, each tracked at two speeds (a slow rating for underlying quality and a fast one for current form), the starting goalie's goals-saved-vs-expected, home ice, rest/back-to-backs and travel (version 4.1, refit every update on 2021-22 onward); if the confirmed goalie differs from the one the Action used, the page re-weights with that goalie's rating. v3 instead fits the de-vigged DraftKings moneyline and total exactly. Each game is simulated with score effects, goalie pulls/empty nets and 3-on-3 OT/shootout, and the pick is the non-tied score with the lowest expected |away error| + |home error| (OT/SO winner gets +1). The OT label is shown when P(OT | that score) ≥ 55%. Without odds the model falls back to team ratings.

Best bet. For every side of every Polymarket market on the game — moneyline, every over/under line (2.5–9.5) and every alt puck line (±1.5, ±2.5, ±3.5), all fetched with Polymarket's includeAllMarkets=true and all settled on the final score including OT/shootout (shootout winner +1 goal) the page asks one question: does this line win if the game ends at the predicted score? Of the lines that do, the best bet is the one that pays the most (the lowest price). Example: a 3–2 home win fits the home moneyline, Over 4.5, Under 5.5 and the away side +1.5; the cheapest of those is the best bet. This is not a value bet. The model's own probability for the line is shown beside it, and the lowest-priced line is usually the least likely of the ones that fit. A line needs a real two-sided quote to count; if none does, the game shows NO BET. The same rules drive the Discord /nhl command.

Polymarket edge. Polymarket NHL game markets (Polymarket-sourced) resolve on the final score including overtime and shootouts, so they are compared with the model's win probability including OT/SO. Price = YES ask (cost of one $1 contract). Edge = model % − Polymarket ask; "no-vig" divides by the sum of both asks. EV per $1 = model / (ask + fee) − 1 with fee = 0.07 × p × (1 − p) per contract (Polymarket's published fee table; rounded up to the cent per order).
