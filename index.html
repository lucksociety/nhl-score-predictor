Summary

How it works. A GitHub Action refreshes the schedule (NHL API), ESPN/DraftKings moneylines and totals, probable goalies and season results every hour from 10 AM to 11 PM ET. When you press Simulate the page also re-pulls live odds and goalies from ESPN, then runs the v3 protocol for every game: the de-vigged moneyline and total are fitted exactly (market weight 1.0), the game is simulated with score effects, goalie pulls/empty nets and 3-on-3 OT/shootout, and the pick is the non-tied score with the lowest expected |away error| + |home error| (OT/SO winner gets +1). The OT label is shown when P(OT | that score) ≥ 55%. Without odds the model falls back to team ratings (weaker: backtest MAE ≈2.84 vs 2.79).

For entertainment and contest use. Not betting advice.
