How it works. A GitHub Action refreshes the schedule (NHL API), ESPN/DraftKings moneylines and totals, probable goalies, Jupiter prediction-market prices and season results every hour from 10 AM to 11 PM ET. When you press Simulate the page also re-pulls live odds and goalies from ESPN and live prices from Jupiter, then runs the v3 protocol for every game: the de-vigged moneyline and total are fitted exactly (market weight 1.0), the game is simulated with score effects, goalie pulls/empty nets and 3-on-3 OT/shootout, and the pick is the non-tied score with the lowest expected |away error| + |home error| (OT/SO winner gets +1). The OT label is shown when P(OT | that score) ≥ 55%. Without odds the model falls back to team ratings.

Jupiter edge. Jupiter NHL game markets (Polymarket-sourced) resolve on the final score including overtime and shootouts, so they are compared with the model's win probability including OT/SO. Price = YES ask (cost of one $1 contract). Edge = model % − Jupiter ask; "no-vig" divides by the sum of both asks. EV per $1 = model / (ask + fee) − 1 with fee = 0.07 × p × (1 − p) per contract (Jupiter's published fee table; rounded up to the cent per order).
