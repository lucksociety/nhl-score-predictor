## Concrete input failure and correction

Four of six starters were labeled Expected. Utah's actual starter was Sebastian Cossa, while the delivered forecast named Karel Vejmelka. The other five starters matched. Previous sensitivity checked scoring scale and skater lineups, but never tested an alternative starting goalie.

`v4_update.py` now stores available current-roster goalie identities and strictly prior-history quality for unconfirmed teams. Injured/unavailable goalies and skaters are excluded. A rookie with no goalie history has a clearly labeled league prior, not invented individual skill. A confirmed goalie is locked.

The simulator tests endpoints of each side's roster-goalie quality range, including simultaneous switches, against the central and existing skater scenarios. Probabilities and displayed point forecasts do not change. A recommendation must retain probability >50% and positive fee/quote-stressed EV in these scenarios. The policy is versioned `scoring-quote-fee-goalie-depth-v3` for later prospective comparison. Discord explains the goalie sensitivity when used, and saved predictions record its results.

This is input robustness, not a calibrated confidence interval or demonstrated win-rate improvement. Cossa's prior quality was worse than Vejmelka's under this model; naming him correctly would not automatically have reversed the Colorado forecast. We do not claim the patch would have prevented either loss.
