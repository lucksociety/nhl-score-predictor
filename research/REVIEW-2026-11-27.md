# NHL input identity and prospective scoring diagnostics — November 27

## Corrections deployed

The expected starting goalie's full name now resolves to a unique **current NHL roster player ID** before historical abbreviated names are consulted. Previously an ambiguous first-initial/surname match could silently choose the goalie with the most career shots; a goalie without stored appearances could also fall back to the team's old goalie blend. A rookie now retains the real roster ID and receives the existing league-prior quality. Historical names cannot override a contradictory current roster. An unresolved named starter remains forecastable with the existing blend but explicitly withholds bets pending identity reconciliation; no player quality is invented.

This is an input-integrity correction, not evidence of higher win rate. Current-roster exact full names take priority over ambiguous initials, skaters are excluded, and full first-name conflicts are not guessed. Existing availability, goalie scenarios, raw >50% probability, positive after-fee/quote-stressed EV, score consistency and executable-depth requirements remain in force.

Each fresh simulation also preserves its exact central 80% total/margin quantiles, using uncapped final-score counts including the OT/SO winner's goal. These are predictive ranges, not confidence intervals on model probabilities. They do not change simulated scores, the RNG, rates or selection. Daily evaluation now records their observed coverage and width, signed total/margin bias and score RMSE alongside existing MAE/log loss. Seven- and 30-day views expose deterioration that an all-time average can hide. Small samples remain visible as small samples; there is no automatic coefficient change or promise of 80% empirical coverage.

The immutable ledger adds schema 2 only when an original forecast contains ranges. Existing schema-1 records and hashes remain valid. Old forecasts are not assigned retrospective ranges; modified ranges fail hash validation. Exact schema-1/schema-2 copies of the same freeze are deduplicated at grading, with richer metadata used only when probability and expected-score content agree.

## Goalie-recency challenger: rejected

Tested the existing goalie rating against 30- and 60-appearance exponential half-lives, retaining the same validated goalie-game xGA, league prior, seasonal carry, team features and simulator. Monthly regressions use strictly earlier games; same-day results cannot enter their own features. Selection is on 2023–24/2024–25 exact-score log loss, with 2025–26 confirmation and 2026–27 through November 21 separately reported. November 22+ losses were excluded. The **baseline won selection** across the 4,308-game replay; faster reaction was not promoted.

These years were inspected in earlier research, so this is exploratory rather than a pristine holdout. Actual historical starters also provide more information than a morning live scan. No historical ROI claim is made. The JSON preserves the unfavorable candidate results, history hash and sample sizes. The research script requires the deterministic production-simulator grid generated by `uncertainty-grid-nov23.js`; the large grid/rate caches are intentionally excluded.
