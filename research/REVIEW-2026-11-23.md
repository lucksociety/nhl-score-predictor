## New rule: probability-value-score-depth-v4

Each candidate must have raw probability >50%, remain above 50% after stored scoring/skater/goalie scenarios and Monte Carlo error, and retain positive fee-adjusted EV at a one-cent worse ask. The line must win at the displayed score, have a fresh two-sided full-game OT/SO quote, and sufficient exact-ask order-book depth. Qualifiers rank by conservative probability, then stressed EV. These scenario minima address input sensitivity; they are not calibrated confidence bounds on the true probability.

The same value predicate runs at candidate selection, order-book verification, and Discord publication. Cached booleans cannot authorize an old nonvalue pick. Publication requires affirmative execution verification. Invalid bets are withheld while every supported prediction stays visible. This can mean fewer bets or a pass; probabilities have not been inflated to produce recommendations. Dashboard wording is corrected to match the shared rule, with no layout changes.

Regression tests include all four November 22 sub-50% primaries, a cheap but negative-EV line, scoring disagreements, incorrect fee costs, probability/EV failures under alternative goalies, stale quotes, insufficient depth, and stale cached cards.
