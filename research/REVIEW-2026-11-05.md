## Corrections

A preserved Jupiter quote could remain eligible after a feed outage because bet selection did not check its timestamp. Require a valid timestamp at most five minutes old at selection and publication. Publication uses the individual game's quote time, and suppresses both the primary pick and alternatives when stale.

Full-game simulated outcomes must match verified OT/shootout contract rules. Store settlement confirmation per market, exclude period markets, and reject unknown total directions and non-half-goal lines. Historical records missing settlement evidence cannot generate a fresh recommendation.

Require both model probability above 50% and fee-adjusted EV beyond 1.96 Monte Carlo standard errors. This measures simulation precision only. The five-minute limit is an operational safeguard chosen for quote freshness, with no claim of an accuracy or profit improvement.

Tests cover stale, missing and future timestamps, incompatible rules, period contracts, unknown total directions, malformed lines, threshold probabilities, and publication without mutation of frozen records.
