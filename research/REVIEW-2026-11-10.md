## Reject fragile/execution-unverified edges

An early November 10 card showed COL +1.5 with a sensitivity-adjusted advantage of only about .022 cents per contract above modeled cost, and NJD +2.5 had little room for price drift. These are not historical losses; they expose fragility in the recommendation policy. Published past cards remain unchanged.

Jupiter's [published API fee table](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/prediction#fees) says fees round up to cents. The previous continuous per-contract 0.07*p*(1-p) estimate ignores order rounding. Use the published single-contract rounding as a conservative whole-contract fee allowance. For integer quantities N, ceil_cent(N*f)/N <= ceil_cent(f). The allowance is not an account-specific order quote; fractional/partial fills and platform fees can differ, so this does not guarantee a realized fee or profit. Display it as a fee allowance, not an exact paid fee.

Require positive EV at the minimum scoring-sensitivity probability, beyond sampling error, even if the ask moves one cent against the bettor. Stress cost retains the larger endpoint fee allowance to avoid false robustness when rounded fees step downward. This is an execution-risk reserve, not a probability recalibration or a fitted accuracy claim. Probability still must be strictly above 50%, and the bet must cash at the displayed score.

After simulations finish, check the [live Jupiter order book](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/api-reference/prediction/get-orderbook) for up to four top qualifying candidates per game, in existing conservative-probability order. Only count contracts executable at or below the exact quoted ask, with a current two-sided book within ten cents. Require at least ten contracts, and enough contracts for at least $5 at the ask. Expensive depth one cent above the ask is not counted. Missing books, insufficient depth, source expiry or crossing kickoff withhold the candidate; a lower-ranked qualified candidate may replace it only after its own book check. Alternatives are verified too. No order is created or placed. All reads occur after the scoring simulation and cannot change its outcomes.

The output identifies the new selection policy separately (`scoring-quote-fee-depth-v2`) for daily contract-specific calibration. Scoring probabilities/model coefficients are not re-fit to a tiny slate or selected price screen.
